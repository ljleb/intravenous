# Middle-button vertical zoom before left-to-zoom-in

_Historical, non-normative. Preserved on 2026-11-18 after the owner reversed
the Alt+middle-button zoom direction._

Source and replacement are in
[Node presentation and manual controls](../node_presentation_and_manual_controls_direction.md#lanes-and-reusable-timeline-display).

> The middle-button press selects the zoom axis. With Alt, drag right to zoom
> in vertically or left to zoom out at the cursor's current height.
