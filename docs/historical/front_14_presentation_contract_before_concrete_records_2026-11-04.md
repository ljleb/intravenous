## Preserved status text

> The presentation half is not implemented. The current documents fix its required
> semantics, but they do not yet define the concrete language-neutral registration
> record consumed from a package, the host-selection record for zero or several
> registrations, or the framework default descriptor. Those records must be decided
> before source code can implement `NodePresentation` without inventing a frontend-
> specific or package-ABI-specific contract.

> The `NodePresentation` portion is not implemented. Its required behavior is settled,

> and framework default descriptor are still unspecified. Front 14 cannot be called
> complete until those records are decided and implemented.
